Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 18, 2026

Siran Christian Church ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when you use the Siran Christian Church mobile application ("the App").

1. Information We Collect

Device and Notification Information

The App uses Firebase services to register devices for church notifications. This may involve an automatically generated anonymous Firebase user identifier, Firebase Cloud Messaging registration token, device platform information, and notification registration timestamps.

Locally Stored Information

Favorite devotions and related reading information may be stored locally on your device using the Room database. This information supports convenient access and offline reading.

Advertising Information

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google and its advertising services may process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, and advertising interactions to provide and measure advertisements.

Technical Information

Third-party services integrated into the App may process technical information necessary to provide their services, maintain security, and support App functionality.

2. How We Use Information

  • To register devices for church notifications.
  • To deliver church-related messages and notifications.
  • To provide App features and improve functionality.
  • To store favorite devotions and support offline reading.
  • To display and manage advertisements.
  • To maintain App security and reliability.

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication: For automatically generated anonymous identifiers.
  • Firebase Cloud Firestore: For storing device notification registration information.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: For delivering push notifications.
  • Google AdMob: For displaying advertisements.
  • Blogger: For retrieving publicly available devotional and discipleship content.
  • YouTube: For accessing sermon videos hosted on YouTube.

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Firebase Privacy Information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information. Information may be processed by the service providers identified in this policy as necessary to operate App features, advertising, notifications, and technical services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when necessary to protect security and legal rights.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the App. These include using Firebase services, secure network connections where supported, and Firebase security rules to restrict access to stored information.

Favorite devotions stored locally remain on your device. However, no electronic storage or internet transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Information is retained only as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App's services and fulfill the purposes described in this policy.

Notification registration information may remain in Firebase until it is updated, removed, or no longer needed. Favorite devotions remain on your device until deleted through available App functions or until local App data is cleared.

To request deletion of notification registration information and its associated anonymous Firebase identifier, contact us using the information below. Uninstalling the App does not necessarily delete information already stored on Firebase servers.

7. Children's Privacy

The App provides Christian educational and church-related resources. We do not knowingly request children to submit personal information through the App. Parents or legal guardians who have concerns about a child's information may contact us for assistance.

8. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to external websites and services, including our church website and YouTube. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites. Please review their privacy policies before providing information.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable laws, you may request information about data processed about you, request correction or deletion of applicable information, and raise concerns about our privacy practices.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when App features, services, or applicable requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
